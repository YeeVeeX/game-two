## What the current telemetry cannot distinguish (the instrumentation spec)

1. Whose loop is slow, without audio-oracle mining: needs **run_ms** (run
   phase wall span) per seat on the close line → update rate =
   (ticks+stalls)/run_ms per seat, WHO in one subtraction. (The audio
   oracle dies in noDevice mode — engine_pcm=0, probe tonight — and
   mining it is a forensics session, not a glance.)
2. During the worst freeze, was the waiting seat's own loop healthy?
   needs the **coherent worst-run pair**: stall_ms_max together with the
   update count of THAT run. ratio ≈ 16.7 ms/update → local loop healthy,
   peer/path frozen (F6 a/b/c-on-peer); ratio ≫ 16.7 → local loop itself
   froze (c/d local). Today stall_run_max and stall_ms_max may come from
   DIFFERENT runs — the ratio would lie.
3. The negotiated d / link_slow / RTT probe list: known only inside the
   handshake (F5). Log at params-known, both seats, always-on.
4. WHAT eats the limiting seat's frame (sim vs draw vs neither =
   pacing/throttle/display): needs the **frame probe** (T1b) on HIS
   seat — and it must work in SOLO mode too (his solo run isolates his
   machine from lockstep entirely).
