## Top issues (ranked)
1. **Faction/entity ambiguity is the biggest structural leak.** Party, enemies, props, and pickups share square silhouettes in overlapping warm hues: brown ally vs brown enemy (t=0:45.13, t=0:59.47–1:00.10), enemies camouflaged against floor/walls (t=0:11.07, t=0:18.60 ~360,450), enemy-vs-prop unresolvable without motion (t=0:21.93), pink drops reading as magenta enemies (t=0:43.83), pink/teal squares never disambiguated at all (t=0:47.53–0:52.03). *Fix candidate (pre-art):* one hard rule — allies warm, enemies cool (or add a 1px red underline tick to every hostile). ZONE 3's navy floor already proves contrast alone fixes it (t=0:38.40).
2. **The red 3x3 burst means at least four different things.** Player spin AoE (t=0:17.30, t=0:25.97), incoming threat tiles (t=0:18.30), possible death VFX (t=0:44.57), off-player entity of unknown ownership (t=0:27.07–0:27.13), plus friendly-AoE vs took-damage in identical frames (t=0:47.80 vs t=0:50.23). Red also carries the damage vignette. *Fix candidate:* one constant — player-owned effects white/gold, hostile telegraphs red. Single palette decision, clip-wide payoff.
3. **Impact feedback is inconsistent and missing its spine.** Hit-flash exists in most segments (t=0:19.17, t=0:28.37, t=1:08.90) but appears absent on the spin's targets (t=0:26.90, t=0:27.80) and on the opening fight (t=0:02.40–0:05.43 — counter ticks, no visible react); no hitstop or shake confirmed anywhere except the possible HUD smear at t=0:24.93; knockback is sub-perceptual or absent (t=0:18.00–0:18.30, t=1:12.77→1:13.27). *Fix candidate:* universal on-hit contract — 2-frame flash + 2–3px lurch + 2-frame hitstop on every damage event, no exceptions. Verify the spin path specifically.
4. **Kills mostly evaporate.** Silent deletions at t=0:05.80→0:08.20, t=0:26.50→0:27.03, t=0:28.87→0:28.97, t=1:08.63→1:09.30 — the loop's payoff moment is mute, while t=0:44.33–0:44.57 proves the pipeline exists. *Fix candidate:* make the t=0:44 chain the default death path for all enemies; scale the faint t=0:24.40 particles 3–4x.
5. **The boss is a text string.** "BOSS 1 SPAWNED" fires with the boss off-screen and no direction indicator (t=1:06.53–1:08.30, first visible t=1:10.63), and the boss itself is a trash-identical beige square findable only by its floating label (t=1:11.17). *Fix candidate (structure, not art):* 2x2-tile footprint + unique color + screen-edge arrow tied to the banner.
6. **Banner pile-up and pacing whiplash at the climax.** Four center-screen banners in ~5.5s, two overlapping, while combat continues under them (t=1:01.00–1:06.53, -6 tick at t=1:03.33); ZONE 4→5 crossed in ~2.3s with identical banner treatment (t=1:03.97 vs 1:06.27); contrast with the dead-air commute just before (t=0:52.33–0:59.17, pink group never engaged). *Fix candidate:* reserve center-screen for zone names only; move TOLL PAID / BOSS SPAWNED to a side ticker; pull the stranded pink group onto the descent route.
7. **Enemy intent is invisible — damage can't be dodged, only absorbed.** No windup/telegraph before contact damage (bar shrink at t=0:21.93 unattributable; "which of three adjacent enemies hit me" unanswerable at t=0:50.23), and enemy projectiles are ~6px low-contrast squares (t=1:12.00–1:12.53). Both spin engagements cost HP (t=0:26.90, t=0:27.80), suggesting attrition rather than skill trades. *Fix candidate:* 1–2 frame squash-lean windup on enemy attacks + 2x projectile size with saturated color. This unlocks the entire reactive layer.
8. **Damage-number semantics are muddled.** "U PROVISION -5" reads as noise mid-scrum (t=0:00–0:07.77); the -40 at t=0:30.33 is unattributable between combat and toll (TOLL PAID fires 300ms later); shop, toll, and combat share one small purple style (t=0:33.27, t=0:35.10). *Fix candidate:* three-color typography rule — red taken / white dealt / gold economy — plus mark which HP bar is the possessed character (unclear at t=0:26.90).
