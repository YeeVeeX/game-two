## Q1 — why the biology frame failed (models converged)

Egyptian New Kingdom sacred logic is SOLAR and transcendent — ascent,
judgment, preservation through order (ma'at), hierarchical distance (gods
withdrawn, monsters liminal). Nest-biology is CHTHONIC and immanent — womb,
eating, growth, biomass. "Metastasis" reads as Carrion/Zerg body-horror, not
dread mythology; it collapses the sacred-profane distance the fiction needs.
No awe. (kimi round 1; glm round 1 concurs: "biopunk, not servants of
withdrawn gods awaiting tribute.")

## Q1 — directions surfaced

1. RITUAL/OFFERING (kimi's lead): banking = weighing offerings; spending buys
   divine ATTENTION (gods notice, never love). First spend: scar a body with
   a god's mark. Endgame: the Weighing of the Heart / becoming the test
   itself. Risk: abstraction — solve with visible omens.
2. NAME-WRITING / PERSISTENCE (glm's original): banked value buys
   PERSISTENCE. Pay to INSCRIBE a body; inscribed bodies survive the vat
   (wipes) and accumulate history/quirks; unnamed bodies are fodder that
   dissolves. Hook: "name your dead." Egyptian name-as-soul (ren);
   erasure-as-damnation. Risk: attachment domesticates dread; early-naming
   min-max.
3. FINAL-ROUND SYNTHESIS (both models): INSCRIPTION WITHIN THE RITUAL FRAME —
   "persistence through judgment." Spend to inscribe bodies with god-marks;
   unmarked bodies dissolve on wipe; framed as devotional obligation (gods as
   creditors), NOT pet-naming. "Name your dead BEFORE they die."
4. Rejected by the debate: war-materiel (map-unlock treadmill, power-fantasy
   drift), pack-capability progression (power creep, grindy), debt-to-
   something-older (spreadsheet feel).

## Q2 — human counterplay in A2: UNANIMOUS none

- Single-variable discipline: A2 tests whether spatial pressure + targeting
  makes bank-or-push meaningful; counterplay confounds it.
- Sequencing argument (glm): counterplay requires established competence —
  A2 teaches the player to fear the field first. The A3 "Challenger" (a
  named human who taunts back) lands harder after five increments of
  "humans never fought back — until one did."
- If ever forced (kimi): scatter > taunt — tests herd management (new
  skill), environmental not personal, fails gracefully. Taunt is binary
  punishment on the already-taught possession skill.
- Pre-register for the future increment: named-nemesis Challenger beat,
  fairness ladder mandatory (visible tell + counters).

## Dev-of-record read

The inscription-within-ritual synthesis is the strongest candidate this
project has produced for "what does the pile buy": fiction-native at the
deepest level (ren; the bible corpus is literally New Kingdom/Amarna — name
erasure is the period's own damnation), original (persistence as the
commodity; no touchstone has it; not a shop), and it aims the spend at the
thing the player already almost loves (the 3-body pack, the last-body
spiral). It converts wipes from "free respawn" to "the unpaid dissolve" —
weight without Tibia extremes (loss touches what you didn't sanctify, never
progression). Caveats to carry into D1b design: session-only persistence
first (restart persistence stays parked per D0), anti-min-max shaping, and
kimi's dread warning (inscribed bodies must remain judgeable, not safe).
