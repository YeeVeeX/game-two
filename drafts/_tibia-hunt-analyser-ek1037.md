## Design reading (folded into the fight-ledger spec)

1. **The analyser is not a loss-teller — it is an honest scale that skilled,
   balanced play BEATS.** The gamesmith extracts emphasize observed negative
   balances (design-judgment §48/§103); this corrects the sample: a
   well-specced character at an appropriate spot runs strongly positive, and
   the GREEN number is the mastery readout. For the beat: green must read as
   EARNED, red as honest — the instrument's job is truth in both directions,
   not manufactured pain. (Corpus praise line already says this: "teaching
   supply-vs-yield mastery as a real skill" — Challenge/Submission.)
2. **Session-level reconciliation is the emotionally load-bearing readout** —
   this screenshot IS the bank-moment P&L in spirit (+1,247k after costs).
   Supports the fun-lens HIGH-2 fold: the leg/bank-granularity tally is the
   touchstone's actual beat, not only the per-fight print.
3. **Per-species kill itemization is first-class analyser grammar** (403x /
   22x / 15x / 366x). At current content (rushers only) fight-level species
   itemization is meaningless — note for when a second hostile kit ships.
4. **Owner design-philosophy quote (2026-08-11, verbatim):** *"the hunt
   results depend on the character specs, equipment, imbuiments, place of
   hunt (monster variety/type) and player expertise of how to position,
   pull, combo, etc, plus other factors, that is the magic of Tibia."*
   Reading: the P&L number is magic because it is downstream of a DECISION
   STACK the player owns. game-two currently feeds the number almost no
   player-owned variables (one hostile kit, no specs/equipment, no spot
   choice). The owner's own list maps to the parked queue: "place of hunt /
   monster variety" = A3 district progression; "how to position, pull" = A2
   pull economy (pre-queued per v8) + shipped taunt. The ledger is the
   INSTRUMENT that makes those variables legible when they arrive — it is
   infrastructure for the magic, not the magic itself. Recorded so LB-1's
   verdict is read at the right altitude.
