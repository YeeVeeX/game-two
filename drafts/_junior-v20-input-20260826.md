## 2. The ask: new dungeons WITH bosses

> "falta novos dungeons com boss"

The strongest single want. Today the world has one real dungeon
(dungeon_1, level-6 gate) and one boss (BOSS 1); the frontier rung
(zone_8, level 8) leads to no boss-capped payoff, and past seal 4
there is no fight that spends the pack's full-grown power. Touchstone:
Tibia's identity is exactly this ladder — hunt zones feeding into
riskier dungeons with named bosses at the bottom (the shelf's
risk-geography thread; also the parked "corpse-run gear drop"
tension multiplier reads best in boss rooms). Kethral precedent:
BSP dungeon layouts stay parked, but AUTHORED dungeons via the WB
pipeline (T1-T5, all shipped) are proven cheap — basements + dungeon_1
took one session each at content-fill (s69).

## 3. World variety — positive verdict, keep the method

> "eu gostei, o mundo está diversificado e legal"

The zone-palette + landmark + content-fill discipline (s68-s70) is
WORKING on the player it was built for. No change asked; recorded so
v20 doesn't fix what isn't broken.

## 4. zone_8 reads empty FOR ITS SIZE

> "parece vazio no mapa novo pelo seu tamanho"

zone_8 is 64x40 — the biggest map in the game — and its density
does not scale with its area. Concrete v20 candidate: a zone_8
content-fill pass (the s69 wave's exact playbook: pockets, landmarks,
interior doors, stations where design allows) and/or carving its far
reaches into gated sub-areas so the emptiness becomes anticipation
instead of dead air. Pairs naturally with 2: the frontier's size is
begging for a dungeon entrance.

## 5. Kit balance at the cap — blocker strong, others fragile (HYGIENE-BANKED)

> "eu sinto que somente o blocker é forte, os outros são meio fracos;
> acho que no nv 10, para equilibrar a balança, eles tinham que ter um
> ataque mais forte, pq eles apanham e morrem com poucos hits"

Read carefully: this is a DAMAGE ask for striker/lobber at high
level, not a survivability ask — he accepts they die fast, but wants
their output to justify the glass. **Measurement hygiene:** difficulty
and progression numbers are exactly what the EIGHTEENTH ritual
measures, and its questions are frozen/virgin — this line is banked
as pre-ritual seat feedback and MUST NOT move `data/balance/*` before
the ritual verdict lands. At the v20 brainstorm it becomes evidence
for a "per-kit growth curves" fork (today growth is uniform integer
damage/HP; a v20 candidate is letting striker/lobber damage scale
steeper than blocker's so the cap feels like three different powers,
not one tank and two liabilities).
