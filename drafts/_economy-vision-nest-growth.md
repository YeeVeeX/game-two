## The thesis: the economy is a biological growth loop, not a gold economy

You are not a hero with a bank account. You are a nest-organism that grows
bodies from a vat. The value you strip from humans is not money — it is
substrate the nest metabolizes. The loop, end state:

    HARVEST (take value from humans, carry it at risk)
    -> FEED (bring it home; "banking" is the nest's MOUTH, a meal not a deposit)
    -> GROW (the nest converts fed value into capability: re-grown bodies,
       organs, capacity)
    -> EXPAND (A3: the nest advances — breaking into a new district and
       re-homing costs a massive fed investment; territory is the aspirational
       purchase. Not property. Metastasis.)

Why banking feels hollow today: ONLY THE MOUTH EXISTS. Deposits with no
metabolism behind them are a score counter. The fix is not redesigning the
mouth (stations, carry, ledger are fun-verified substrate — Q1 "landed as a
payoff") — it is building the body behind it, increment by increment.

## The three loops (core concepts, smallest possible set)

1. THE FIGHT (seconds) — position-pressure combat: the box closes or you
   shape it. Pull-sizing by movement. Legible, learnable threat (Darklight
   Core finding: predictable threat invites denser play; chaos caps it).
   A2 delivers this loop.
2. THE EXPEDITION (minutes) — walk out empty; hunt deeper along the gradient;
   the carry grows; threat contests position, corpse, and the walk home;
   bank-or-push. THE HOOK LIVES HERE: our per-pull greed lever is original —
   the corpus itself flags that no touchstone demonstrates a moment-to-moment
   "one more pull" temptation (Tibia's depth choice is session-scale spot
   selection). A2 + ledger deliver the tension; D1b gives the return meaning.
3. THE NEST (sessions) — fed value accumulates into visible growth and,
   eventually, territory. THE SERIOUS VIBE LIVES HERE: permanence, weight,
   the sunk-cost anchor (studio digest: hours invested into ACCESS are the
   retention engine — threatened access hurts more than lost items).

## What we take from Tibia — grammar, not sentences

KEEP the structure: risk has prices, sessions are expeditions not attendance,
threat is learnable rules, death is dreaded not routine.
REFUSE the extremes: no XP/skill loss, no equipment loss, no hours-long
recovery grind. Loss in game-two is always the EXPEDITION's harvest (carried
pile, corpse, time), never PROGRESSION. That is the "not as extreme" line.

ORIGINAL to us (not in Tibia or the corpus):
- Monster flip: you are the ecology; humans are the visitors.
- Per-pull greed lever (moment-to-moment push-your-luck).
- Economy as biology: feeding/growth/metastasis, not gold/shops.
- Death as attrition arc: the 3->2->1 body spiral is a tension ramp no
  single-avatar game has; the wipe is its crescendo.
