### BLOCKING findings (Check 1)

**B1 — Night-hour/chamber arithmetic cannot cohere with midnight-at-the-twelfth plus the return ascent.** §5.1 (~222): "Each of the twelve night-hours corresponds to the chamber the Lantern-barge is passing through" — a 1:1 hour↔chamber mapping over a twelve-hour night puts chamber 12 at the final night-hour, adjacent to dawn. But the very next sentence (~222): "Midnight, when the Ember touches Ulveth's heart" and §3.3 (~130): "at midnight, in the deepest chamber, she lays the guttering Ember against Ulveth's heart... Then Savra fights her way back up the eastern stair, and if she wins, there is a dawn." Midnight = mid-night, so the deepest (12th) chamber must be reached at hour ~6, leaving hours 7–12 for the ascent — which breaks the stated 1:1 correspondence (descent would run 2 chambers/hour, and ascent hours re-pass chambers 11→1 with no mapping). No reading satisfies all three of: (i) 1:1 hour↔chamber, (ii) deepest at midnight, (iii) ascent completed by dawn. Related route incoherence in §3.3 (~130): the itinerary lists "through the twelve chambers of the buried god, through the outer dark where... Ithves strikes... and at midnight, in the deepest chamber" — the outer-dark leg is placed *after* the twelve chambers yet *before* arrival at the twelfth of them. Note any fix must preserve hour↔chamber flavor: §12.6's "Kammat of the Ninth Hour" (~530) and §13's "Night hours key to Savra's descent through the twelve chambers" (~554) both lean on it.
