# M2.1 feel-repair — diagnosis + work order (2026-08-09)

**Owner verdict on M2 (verbatim):** "game feels slugish now, dash/doge is not very useful
and instead the character gets stuck and the teammates now feel dumb and weak, the enemies
are too hard if the player doesn't have spells or more stronger combos to chain."

**Frame:** M1 was "feels really good"; M2 broke it. So this is a REGRESSION introduced by
M2's changes, not a missing-content problem. **Do NOT add spells/new systems** — that is
the Kethral trap (bury a feel bug under new features). Diagnose → tune → re-verify.
The pincer AI itself was owner-ordered ("make it difficult... more aggressive") and STAYS;
what gets fixed is everything the pincer exposed.

## Root causes (traced in code, priority order)

### 1. Dodge is a NO-OP exactly when you need it (CONFIRMED in code)
`Creature#dodge` → `GridWalker#dash` → `commit` (`grid_walker.rb:55-80`): the dash stops
BEFORE the first blocked tile; if the first tile in the dodge direction is occupied,
`tiles == 0` → returns false → **no movement, no iframes, nothing**. `blocked` = every
other actor's tile. The M2 pincer (`surround_slot`) systematically fills the adjacent
ring — the surround claims exactly the tiles a dodge needs. Under M1's follow-each-other
AI there was usually an open lane; under M2 there is not. "Dash/dodge not very useful,
character gets stuck" is this line.

**Fix (dev-of-record call):** dodge dashes THROUGH bodies and lands on the first FREE tile
within range (walls still hard-stop; if no free tile in range, refuse as today). Hades-style
dash-through. Deterministic, no new systems, turns the pincer into a risk-reward puzzle
(dodge through the ring, don't get re-surrounded) instead of a coffin. Implementation:
`dash` gains `through:` (occupied tiles that may be crossed but not landed on) or a
`pass_through: true` mode on commit; melee step behavior unchanged.

### 2. Hitstop fires on RECEIVING hits — pincer turns it into slideshow (HIGH CONFIDENCE)
`World#wire_events`: victim == possessed → `feel.on_player_hit` → 3f hitstop EACH hit.
Five rushers at 66f exhaust each, surround geometry landing 3-5 hits per window when
pincered ≈ 9-15 frozen frames per 66 ≈ **15-25% of wall time frozen** mid-fight. M1's
single-file AI rarely landed simultaneous pressure, so this never showed. "Sluggish" is
mostly this (perf is measured innocent: p95 0.039ms, overrun counter dark).

**Fix:** hitstop only when the possessed DEALS a hit or kills (Vlambeer doctrine: freeze
sells YOUR impact). Receiving keeps the crimson flash + shake 6.0 (readable, not sticky).
Data change in `feel` handling: `on_player_hit` keeps shake, drops hitstop.

### 3. Every Tab kills held movement (CONFIRMED in code)
`PossessedController#rearm!` masks ALL actions held at swap time until released — including
movement. Swap is the core verb; every mid-walk Tab = new body stands still until the key
is re-pressed. Constant micro-stalls read as "sluggish".

**Fix:** unmask MOVEMENT keys on rearm; keep attack/dodge edge-triggered. Law 2's intent
(no ghost-fired attack, no burned dodge cooldown) is fully preserved — held walk continuing
into the new body is what the hand expects.

### 4. Rushers outrun 2 of 3 kits and aggro from farther (CONFIRMED in data)
rusher step 14 vs striker 13 / lobber 16 / blocker 19; rusher aggro 12 vs pack 8/9.
Only the striker can disengage; possessing blocker/lobber = guaranteed engagement forever.
Combined with dead dodge, there is NO defensive verb. My aggression tune overshot on
footspeed.

**Fix:** rusher `step_frames` 14→16, `aggro_tiles` 12→10. Difficulty now comes from the
surround GEOMETRY (kept) not raw footspeed. Windup 20 + damage 12 stay.

### 5. Allies are objectively weaker + passive (CONFIRMED in data + review)
- Aggro asymmetry: pack kits 8/9 vs rusher 12 — allies stand idle while rushers converge.
  → align pack `aggro_tiles` to 10.
- Blocker needs 3 swings (60f exhaust each = ~3s) to kill one 50hp rusher. → damage 20→25
  (2-shot). Keeps identity (slow wall) but pulls weight.
- Lobber AI adjacent to a hostile is INERT (needs dist>=2, review finding, parked to A1) —
  owner verdict "teammates feel dumb" PROMOTES the minimal fix into M2.1: husk-grade rule
  in `AiController#engage` — projectile kit + target adjacent → step AWAY from target
  (first free tile increasing chebyshev). ~6 lines, deterministic. Full retreat/kite
  behavior remains A1 gambit territory.

### 6. "Spells / stronger combos" → PARKING_LOT, not code
The swap IS the combo system (three kits = three verbs). It is currently masked by 1-5:
you can't dodge out, you freeze constantly, swap stalls your feet, and two kits can't
disengage. Fix the mask, re-verify. If the owner still wants more offensive depth AFTER
M2.1 plays well, that's the A1 conversation (gambits) or a scoped A0.5 (e.g. per-kit
special on a long exhaust) — owner call, spec first.

## Execution order (one-bundled-change-set once approved)

2. Feel: drop hitstop from on_player_hit (keep shake). Test: pincer window, assert no

6. `rake` + `rake perf` + BOTH gates. Capture re-pins EXPECTED (rusher speed shifts every

7. Owner re-check, same 4 axes: kit identities / Lobber possession / pincer pressure /
   District One — plus explicitly: "does dodge feel like an escape now?"
