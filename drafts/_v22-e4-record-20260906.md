## Decision: RECORDED LAW AMENDMENT (no pixel changes), + the six zones enter the contract

Why not re-author: the palettes ARE the ratified art. The MEDUSA TOWER (DUNGEON 2–4) was designed
as light stone floor + dark buried-rock red wall (`drafts/_medusa-tower-concepts-20260831.md`, owner
"go"), BRASA (DUNGEON 5–7) as a low-key fire-lit cave carried by lava tiles + fire glows
(`src/app/light.rb`). Junior played both (26-min city session; "o visual me prendeu"), wall #1–#3
gated every tower/brasa reel with `zones_distinct` PASS. Re-authoring to satisfy a value-only law
would (a) undo ratified art, (b) invalidate every tower/brasa pin, (c) fix a number, not a read.

What the v20 contract actually protects: LEGIBILITY of wall vs floor in the FLAT (no-texture)
fallback. Its wording — "wall LIGHTER than floor, luma spread >= 40" — is a VALUE-ONLY,
ONE-ORIENTATION encoding of that goal, true for the pilot's dark-floor zones and for the ratified
`low_quay <-> zone_7` edge, and unable to describe two families the sealed bible added.

## The amended law (`test/game/zone_identity_data_test.rb`)
Two surfaces are legible when EITHER
- **value**: `|luma(wall) − luma(floor)| >= 40` (the v20 law, now orientation-free), OR
- **chroma**: `RGB distance(wall, floor) >= 40` AND `|luma spread| >= 20` (hue carries the read; value
  still helps at small scale and for colour-blind players — a pure-hue equal-value pair is REFUSED).

Orientation is a NAMED choice per zone (`DARK_FLOOR_ZONES` = pilot + brasa; `LIGHT_FLOOR_ZONES` =
tower), asserted by sign: amending the law for the tower cannot silently let a dark-floor zone flip.
Motif law made orientation-free: motif luma inside the floor..wall band, nearer the floor (on a dark
floor a lighter speckle, on the tower's light stone a darker one — both read as texture, not items).
Ambient / decor / landmark / no-gold rows unchanged; the six zones now run under all of them.

## CORRECTION (landing review 2026-09-06, MAJOR 1) — the "chroma" clause measured the wrong thing
The fresh-eyes reviewer showed that Euclidean RGB distance carries the GREY axis: an achromatic pair wall
[124,124,124] / floor [100,100,100] (spread 24, zero hue) passed as "legible by chroma", and BRASA's 41–52 was
value relabelled - its ORTHOGONAL chroma (component of wall−floor perpendicular to grey) is only 15–18. TOWER is
genuinely hue-carried (83–100). The law now has THREE named clauses (`test/game/zone_identity_data_test.rb`):
value |spread| ≥ 40 · chroma (orthogonal) ≥ 40 with |spread| ≥ 20 · **low-key**: 20 ≤ |spread| < 40 AND floor
