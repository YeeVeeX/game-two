ROLE: Skeptical game-design researcher doing independent adversarial review of a MEASUREMENT RITUAL DESIGN. Default to finding what is WRONG or MISSING. If you cannot verify or judge something, say so plainly instead of guessing.

CONTEXT (ground truth, complete):
A 2-player hobby co-op grid ARPG (Ruby+Gosu, deterministic lockstep netplay, host-authoritative persistent save). The only two players are the two owners (Gabriel es-CR, Junior pt-br) who also direct development. Each dev cycle ends with a "fun-verify ritual": N real play sessions, then each player answers a small set of questions independently, verbatim-banked, wording kept VIRGIN (players never see questions before administration). A mechanical half is judged from session logs/bytes only. Pre-registered routing rows map each plausible negative answer to a pre-committed outcome BEFORE sessions run, so bad news cannot be reinterpreted after the fact. Sample size is permanently 2 humans; this is accepted (the game exists only for them).

Caveat 1: "Same-day spacing... The two sessions ran 22:28 -> 22:51 the SAME night, ~23 minutes apart... what the answers can prove is 'session 2 entered the world session 1 left and both players recognized it', not 'the world still felt continuous after a day away'. The across-days form of the question is NOT evidenced by this ritual."
Caveat 2: "Symmetric audio novelty... the free-verdict positives are audio-colored (first coop exposure to the owner-original library, retuned that same day) - read as directionally valid, not clean-room."

THE NEW CYCLE (v19) SHIPS: XP/leveling on the shared pack (kills -> permanent power), stat-driven combat formula, level-gated zones, safe-zone geography (hubs safe, deep dangerous), session-open mercy floor (first revive affordable at home), respawn pacing retune, ally-AI defensive-default fix, enemies walk home instead of freeze-teleport, a non-pausing client menu, stats panel, combat legibility telegraphs.

THE PROPOSED v19 RITUAL SHAPE (the artifact under review, complete):
1. Feel thesis (what "fulfilled" means): both players, independently: growth was FELT (stronger than at cycle open; kills mattered beyond the moment); boss-reach gap closed honestly (attainable-hard, not wall-hard); session-open is not a chore; the third AI body stopped being a lemming; free verdict, always.
2. Two coop sessions on the shared save, DIFFERENT calendar days - hard rule, mechanically checked from log timestamps (fixes caveat 1). Owner compression of the gap = re-buying the caveat, recorded before sessions.
3. Novelty quarantine (fixes caveat 2): no first-exposure sensory batch inside ritual sessions - every major new audio/visual surface gets >=1 ordinary play session per seat BEFORE the ritual; checkable from logs (audio sha / build fingerprint). Unavoidable asymmetry recorded before answers are read.
4. Mechanical half (from banked bytes): save chain digest intact across both sessions; zero desyncs; clean quits; tick floor 36000 per session; at least one strictly-positive progression fact carried and NAMED (level above start / a level-gate crossed); bot-free logs; day-gap check.
5. Felt-half question TOPICS (exact wording written later, frozen at spec, never rehearsed with players): growth-felt; difficulty arc; geography of risk (safe vs deep); third-body behavior; continuity in its across-days form; free verdict. One economy topic is measured by telemetry ONLY, no question (a prior cycle burned that question by explaining the mechanic to a player after his answers).
6. Routing rows pre-registered at spec: every plausible bad answer mapped to a pre-committed outcome before sessions run.
7. Staging: ritual runs at cycle end after all lanes ship; the moment question wording freezes, the sim numbers it measures (progression pacing, difficulty, respawn, sustain) freeze for tuning; economy/AI data moves are sequenced early in the cycle for this reason.
