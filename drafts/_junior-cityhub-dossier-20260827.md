## 3. The three routes

### Route 1 — GROW zone_7 in place

- Shape: expand the 44×28 canvas (or densify without expanding);
  add city services around the existing bank+seal core.
- For: zero home-hub relocation risk (zone_7 ALREADY holds the deep-side
  anchor role, B2/B3); every existing way stays wired; smallest new-map
  surface; the intro arc is untouched by construction.
- Against: zone_7 is a WB-pipeline hand emission — growing the canvas
  reflows every authored tile + the four transitions + wall scripts that
  capture it (town_gates, safe_boundary…): a re-gate wave, not a green
  field. And the cardinal sketch wants the city as a CROSSROADS — zone_7
  sits mid-ladder on the west spine, not at a compass center; growing it
  bends the topology around an off-center hub.
- NEW asks: none mechanically — authoring + re-gates only.

### Route 2 — AUTHOR CITY 1 (new zone, WB pipeline)

- Shape: new hand-authored zone at city scale/density; zone_7 keeps its
  town role; CITY 1 anchors the cardinal crossroads EAST of the current
  world (the owner sketch: the final-area dungeon connects to the next
  city — my J-T2's DUNGEON 2 is positioned to BE that connector:
  zone_8 → DUNGEON 2 → CITY 1, the first city→dungeon→city segment,
  cross-pinned s90).
- For: T1–T5 precedent says one session per zone; total authoring
  control over density (the T26 wall); green-field = no re-gate wave on
  existing maps; the spine grows exactly as the owner sketched, nothing
  discarded.
- Against: biggest hand-authoring bill of the three (city-scale density
  × hand placement); "home" question opens — does home-hub MOVE to
  CITY 1 or stay at camp/zone_7? (fence: grill prices it); two hamlets +
  one city risks zone_7 demotion to corridor (mitigation: zone_7 keeps
  the L4/5/6 ladder — its traffic is structural).
- NEW asks: none mechanically — it is "more zone" through the shipped
  pipeline. (Candidate 9 rung i biome palettes are SAFE-class and ride
  along free if the grill wants the east biome distinct.)

### Route 3 — worldsmith v2 CITY archetype (generated emission)

- Shape: commission the city as a worldsmith archetype (T26 §D H1
  city-cluster hypothesis, CONFIRM/KILL-tagged); hub_spoke cluster with
  exit pins already expresses cardinal topology at cluster level (slate
  dev read (i): "strongest capability↔demand match yet").
- For: the cardinal crossroads is LITERALLY the generator's native
  shape; scales to the next city and the one after (the spine is a
  pattern, not a one-off); the worldsmith seat is idle and chartered.
- Against: the T26 §A number is the caution — zone_8 (their v1
  emission) is exactly the map that reads EMPTY; a generated CITY
  (the densest zone class in the game) bets the hardest density
  problem on the v2 upgrade being real. Intake pins (md5) make every
  post-emission hand-fix a conscious re-pin. The worldsmith v2 grill is
  its OWN grill (triggers met, owner-paced) — route 3 couples two
  grills.
- NEW asks: worldsmith v2 city archetype (their lane); game-side, none.
