## Per-string worst issues (failures only)

### wipe.line — "LA REENCARNACION ES INMINENTE"

- **Accuracy (2/5):** Factually false. Claims reincarnation (cyclic rebirth from a different
  theological tradition). Suvareth's canon is explicit: death is Half-Passage + Re-knotting
  at a shrine, not rebirth. The temples say the recovered soul "has not come back from death;
  it simply never finished dying." The Khelat heresy that taught cyclic return is what BROKE
  the world. Additionally: "es inminente" shifts to system-announcement voice (telling what
  WILL happen) rather than the Court's recording voice (stating what HAS happened). EN canon
  "THE FLESH IS SPENT" is a true court-voice statement about the body's state.
- **Register (2/5):** Clinical bulletin register ("X es inminente"), not court-stamp. The other
  stamps use notarial "queda + participle" formulas; this one shifts to declarative/descriptive
  mode. "Reencarnacion" is a dharmic-tradition loan that clashes with the fiction's specific
  death-vocabulary (re-knotting, the Half-Passage, the Toll). Fails the slop test — any
  respawn-mechanic game could ship this line unchanged.
- **Slop-consistency (2/5):** Breaks both theology and register. "Reencarnacion" contradicts the
  canon's re-knotting/Half-Passage mechanic, the predictive voice breaks from the court's
  past-settled stamp register, and the line is genre-generic (any respawn game ships this).

### challenger.called.line — "ALGO HA DESPERTADO"

- **Accuracy (2/5):** Does not say what is happening. The actual game event: Varekka is chanting
  the player's body's making-name (Law II name-magic). The player needs to learn: YOUR flesh is
  the target, interrupt or lose it. The ES line communicates none of this — wrong subject (vague
  "algo" vs. your flesh), wrong verb (awakened vs. called/named), wrong teaching (no urgency
  signal, no interrupt cue). Says something untrue about what the game event IS.
- **Register (3/5):** Natural Spanish, zero translationese, third-person event recording —
  technically court-compatible. But "ALGO" (something) is deliberately vague in a world whose
  operating system is NAMING; the court is exact, this is atmospheric. Borderline slop-test:
  the most generic dark-fantasy line in Spanish.
- **Slop-consistency (1/5):** Textbook failure. "Algo ha despertado" ships in every Souls-like,
  every dark-fantasy game, every horror title unchanged. Drops all fiction-specific content the
  EN carries. The court records vague observation ("something") instead of precise event —
  Suvareth's court knows the name of everything it stamps; vagueness is out of character.

### zone.low_quay — "El Bajofondo"

- **Accuracy (4/5):** Loses the explicit quay/dock/barge dimension. "Bajofondo" carries "low
  depths" and (in River Plate usage) "seedy port-side underworld" — which maps to "under-harbor
  where looters strip the dead." Not factually wrong, but trades one meaning-layer (harbor
  infrastructure) for another (depths/underworld).
- **Slop-consistency (3/5):** Weakest zone name. "Bajofondo" is a real Spanish word already in
  wide cultural use (shallows, underworld slang, the tango band). Captures "low" and "under"
  but loses the quay/dock/harbor specificity. Passes narrowly because the zone set's overall
  cohesion carries it, but this one COULD ship in a noir or crime game unchanged.

### Owner-ratified overrides (dev objection on record)

**wipe.line** ("LA REENCARNACION ES INMINENTE") and **challenger.called.line** ("ALGO HA
DESPERTADO") carry owner-ratified overrides. The scores above reflect honest per-axis
judgment: wipe.line scores 2/2/2 and challenger.called.line scores 2/3/1. The dev's
objection is that both strings make false claims about the fiction's mechanics (wipe.line
invokes a cosmology that does not exist and whose historical precedent broke the world;
challenger.called.line drops the name-magic teaching that the beat exists to deliver) and
both fail the slop test (genre-generic lines that could ship in any dark-fantasy game
unchanged). These scores are reported honestly per the axis-scoring instructions. **The
ship decision on these two strings is the owner's recorded call, not the gate's.** The
gate passes the SET because the two failures are isolated and the remaining 21 strings
establish the voice; it does not pass these individual strings.
