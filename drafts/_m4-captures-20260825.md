## Set 1 — `captures/service_toll_during_banner/` (27 frames, 960x540)

Script: `harness/service/toll_during_banner.json` (seed 7; start =
basement_2, banked 40, level 5 — toll_pocket family). Staged honestly,
authored via a live pilot session then transcribed verbatim:

1. Boot in basement_2 (nest+basement banners at f0 both dead by f300).
2. Clear the west room: 2 husks die f146/f147 (kill XP 16 — no level
   ding; drops rest at [3,4]/[4,4], term ~1680f, no expiry flash in
   any window).
3. Exit up the stairs → zone_7 f337; dwell 210f so the ZONE 7 banner
   dies clean.
4. **Re-enter basement_2 f547 (`zone_entered`)** → BASEMENT 2 banner
   alone on the queue, nominal window f547–697.
5. Waypointed walk [4,4]→[5,4]→[5,3]→[5,2]→[6,2] (avoids the [4,3]
   stairs), **seal press on the station tile: `seal_breached` f656**
   (banked 40→0, cost 40) — toll d=+109 inside the 150f window,
   banner dwelling in every toll frame.
6. Idle at [6,2] through banner death. No pocket crossing (see
   constraint note); pocket hostiles are wall-quarantined and never
   engage; the possessed is never hit (no hurt vignette anywhere).

**Geometry note (why basement_2):** map 12 tiles = 384px < 960 view →
camera clamps to (0,0), so the view-centered banner/breach bands
(x≈407–553) sit over void — no sprite can ever touch them. In
dungeon_1/zone-width maps the camera centers the presser under the
band (row-2 seals put the possessed's sprite inside the rule pair) —
verified live in a discarded dungeon staging; basement_2 is the only
current seal whose toll frames are collision-free by construction.

**Constraint (recorded, not attempted-and-faked): M3-2's
"queue handoff during breach fade" is unstageable honestly today.**
Math: breach fade = final third ⇔ breach age ≥ 100 at banner death ⇔
toll ≤ entry+50. Min honest entry→seal walk: basement_2 4 steps
(~76f) + press ≈ d 80+ (we measured d=109 with a tight scripted
walk); dungeon_1 3 steps ≈ d 60+ AND its frames are
camera-contaminated (above). A real queue handoff additionally needs
a second banner enqueued in-window; the only in-reach trigger is the
pocket self-transition, and crossing it inside the expiry window
drags a camera cut + 5 pocket hostiles (hurt-vignette risk) into the
evidence frames. So the delivered expiry bracket shows
slot-active→slot-empty under a dwelling breach line; handoff-during-
breach-fade remains mock-only evidence on their side. If they want
the noisy pocket-handoff variant anyway (handoff during breach DWELL,
fight in lower-left, band-safe by the geometry note), it is one ask
away — the pilot route is banked.
