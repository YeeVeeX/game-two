(`App::AudioBridge::LIB_ROOT = ../game-two-audio`), per the audio seat's
own recommendation: same-machine program pair, no gemspec exists today;
packaging happens if distribution ever demands it. The DLL loads from the
LIBRARY's vendor tree (native.rb resolves its own path — no second copy

seats never boot audio. Committed: `src/app/audio_bridge.rb` (bridge),
`src/main.rb` (SDL_AUDIODRIVER=dummy at process entry + boot),
`src/app/window.rb` (attach where the world is born; update after tick;
shutdown before window close — 186 lines, cap holds), `src/app/cli.rb`
