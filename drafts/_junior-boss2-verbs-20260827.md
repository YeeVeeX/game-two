# J-T5 — BOSS 2 verb concepts (Junior seat, 2026-08-27)

Owner-released batch (2026-08-27). Closes my own J-T1 dossier fork 3:
"seize is taken — what does the second boss threaten?" Three verb
concepts, each contrasted against BOSS 1's anatomy, each with its
telegraph/counterplay/legibility read. Paper-only: concepts and
mechanisms, ZERO numbers (every frames/tiles value is BOSS 1 citation or
deferred). The grill picks one (or kills all three); the kit entry is a
v20 build ticket after that.

## 0. What BOSS 1 already owns (contrast baseline, read live)

`challenger` kit: chant (telegraphed wind-up) → SEIZE a pack body at
range (body theft; the player LOSES a body for a duration; swap-escape
is the counterplay) + big aggro radius. Emotional register: **"he takes
your people."** Counterplay verb: swap. Arena preference: open ground
(seize range wants line of sight). Whatever BOSS 2 is, it must not read
as "seize again" — different resource threatened, different counterplay
verb exercised, different arena shape wanted (J-T2's sealed 2-wide-mouth
room is the stage; the verb should USE that stage).

## Concept A — THE WARDEN: arena control (threatens POSITION)

- Verb: the boss periodically claims GROUND — telegraphed floor zones
  inside the arena become hostile bands (deny-areas) for a window; the
  fight is a shrinking-safe-ground dance around the boss.
- Register: "he takes your ROOM." Counterplay verb: movement/dodge —
  the one verb no fight currently examines under pressure.
- Legibility: floor-band telegraphs are shipped grammar (volley
  brackets, respawn tells, telegraph frames — the visual family
  exists); a new tint band is SAFE-class vocabulary + one draw pass.
- Mechanism honesty: needs a "hostile ground damages/pushes" rule —
  **SIM-class tile-adjacent behavior, the one-gated-piece law applies**
  (same class as lava; candidate 9's fence). This is concept A's real
  price and the grill should see it named.
- Arena fit (J-T2): PERFECT — a sealed room with one mouth is exactly
  where ground-denial forces movement instead of door-camping; the
  2-wide mouth stops the degenerate "fight him in the corridor" out.
- Risk: overlaps volley's impact-tile grammar visually — needs its own
  tint family (the D11/edge-pip lesson: every cue family distinct).

## Concept B — THE TOLLKEEPER: threatens the CARRIED VALUE

- Verb: a telegraphed grab/pulse that RIPS carried (unbanked) value off
  pack bodies in range and visibly HOARDS it on the boss; killing the
  boss returns the pile (his drop = what he took, plus his own table).
  Fleeing the fight forfeits what he holds.
- Register: "he takes your LOOT." Counterplay: bank discipline BEFORE
  entering (the economy loop teaching itself) + range control during.
- Legibility: carried-value grammar is the game's most developed system
  (carried counter, drops, corpse loads, banked/carried split — D0-D1b
  lineage); "the boss holds your money" is one hoard readout over his
  head (price-halo family, shipped).
- Mechanism honesty: value transfer between actors EXISTS (corpse
  looting, drops); an "actor steals carried" event is new SIM logic but
  composes from shipped pieces — no new tile/floor class. Cheaper than
  A in SIM surface.
- Arena fit: good — the sealed room means you CHOSE to bring your
  carry in; the toll reads as the room's rule (J-T2's "boss is a paid
  choice" discoverable rule, doubled).
- Risk: economy-adjacent — the ritual measures sustain/economy
  questions, so this concept MUST wait for the verdict reading (R-E
  row) before the grill prices it; punishing carry may fight the
  "bank now or push deeper" tension D0 already verified as core fun —
  it sharpens that tension, but the grill should check it doesn't
  DOMINATE it.

## Concept C — THE SEALBREAKER: threatens the DUNGEON STATE itself

- Verb: on a chanted cycle, the boss RE-SEALS the arena door (or
  re-arms the dungeon's seal) unless interrupted — the fight has a
  clock shaped like the dungeon's own grammar; letting him finish
  costs the pack the toll again (re-open) or resets the approach.
- Register: "he takes the DOOR." Counterplay: interrupt-on-chant —
  the exact skill varekka already teaches (chant → interrupt window),
  graduated: BOSS 1 chant steals a body, BOSS 2 chant steals progress.
- Legibility: seal/breach grammar is loud and shipped (TOLL PAID
  stamps, seal states, breach banners); "the door re-seals" is a state
  flip the player already reads.
- Mechanism honesty: seal state mutation by an ACTOR is new sim logic
  (seals today mutate only by player payment) — one new event path,
  no new tile class. Between A and B in SIM surface.
- Arena fit: the sealed door IS the stage prop — the room's entry
  mechanism becomes the fight's stake. Strongest thematic unity with
  J-T2.
- Risk: frustration shape — losing the door mid-fight could read as
  "pay the toll twice", which is punishment-by-repetition; mitigation
  shape (named only): the re-seal threatens the SHORTCUT, never the
  only exit.

## Seat recommendation (defended, not decided)

**C first, A second, B parked-with-trigger.** C exercises the skill the
game already teaches (chant-interrupt) at a higher stake, has the
strongest unity with the DUNGEON 2 design, and its SIM ask is one event
path. A is the better FIGHT but carries the tile-class law price — right
choice if the grill wants to spend a gated SIM piece on it. B waits for
the ritual's economy reading by necessity (its own risk row) — park it
with the R-E trigger, not against it.
