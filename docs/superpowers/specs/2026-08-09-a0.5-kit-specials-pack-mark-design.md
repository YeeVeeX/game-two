## Why this increment (owner directive, 2026-08-09)

Owner verdict after fun-verifying M2.1: *"feels better now, yeah, now lets work on the combat
mechanics itself, add some spells and methods of teamwork."* Brainstorm converged on:
**one special ability per kit** (big-moment cadence, ~10s) designed to chain ACROSS bodies via
the swap, plus **one pack command** (focus-target mark). Gambit engine stays A1. This is A0.5:
smaller than a full A-increment, bigger than a tune.

**Fun thesis:** *the swap becomes a combo system you can feel.* Special exhausts are
per-creature and swap-inert (law 4), so the natural line is cast → Tab → cast → Tab → cast — a
three-beat the architecture already supports invisibly. Specials make it visible and loud;
the mark makes the allies part of the plan instead of scenery.

## The three specials

All numbers are **hypotheses** → `data/balance/combat.json` under `kits.<kit>.special`
(zero constants in code, non-negotiable #3). 600f = 10s at the 60fps tick.

| | Blocker: **Slam** | Lobber: **Volley** | Striker: **Lunge** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role in the chain | Opener — control | Zone denial — punish | Finisher — execute |
| Effect | Hits all 8 ring tiles; 2-tile knockback; 45f stagger AND windup-interrupt on every human hit | 3 impact tiles at distances 2/3/4 along facing; telegraphed 40 sim-frames; then AoE damage per tile | Dashes 4 tiles THROUGH bodies via the shared dash plan; damages every human crossed; i-frames for the dash |
| Damage | 30 (control, not kill — Slam's payload is the interrupt+stagger, not DPS) | 35 per tile | 50 (one-shots a rusher — the finisher fantasy) |
| Cast frames | windup 12 / active 4 / recovery 12 | windup 10 / recovery 12 (impact is remote) | windup 6 / dash 4f-per-tile |
| Special exhaust | **600f** | **720f** | **480f** |

**Staggered exhausts are load-bearing (review finding FA):** three equal 600f clocks
re-synchronize after every chain, making L-Tab-L-Tab-L a rote 10s rotation. 480/600/720
means the chain never fully re-aligns — "which special is up" stays situational, and the
faster kit recharging faster reinforces identity.

**Casting rules — the action spine (review finding F1; the draft's "composes cleanly" was
wrong):** the existing state machine hardcodes `kit[:attack]` frames, has a single-victim
per-swing boolean, and `World#resolve_attacks` reads `kit[:attack][:damage]/[:arc]`
unconditionally. A0.5 generalizes it:
- `Creature` gains a **`current_action`** (`:attack` or `:special`); windup/active/recovery
  frame counts, damage, arc/tiles, and knockback come from the ACTION's data, resolved by
  `World#resolve_attacks` through the action — never from `kit[:attack]` directly.
- The per-swing hit registry becomes **per-victim** (a cast may damage each victim once;
  Slam needs 8, the old boolean allowed 1).
- Stagger blocks starting either action; `interrupt_on_hit` interrupts specials exactly as
  it does attacks (striker/lobber yes, blocker no — kit identity carries).
- A special cannot start unless `attack_state == :idle`, and basic attack cannot start
  mid-special (one shared state machine, two action kinds).
- **Slam's interrupt override (review finding FE):** `Creature#stagger!` blocks NEW verbs
  but does not stop an in-flight windup, and rushers are `interrupt_on_hit: false` — an
  already-telegraphing rusher would land its hit THROUGH the Slam, falsifying the control
  identity. Slam therefore explicitly resets each hit human's `attack_state` to `:idle`,
  overriding `interrupt_on_hit`. The override IS the special.
- **Special exhaust is a SECOND per-creature clock**, independent of attack exhaust —
  creature-owned, swap-inert, ticks while unpossessed (law 4 verbatim). Basic attacks stay
  available while special recharges. **`Creature#revive!` resets it to READY** (review
  finding FB — the wipe already costs the run; a wipe must never be a cooldown refresh
  strategy, and it isn't: it resets ALL clocks including this one).
- Specials hit hostiles only (no friendly fire anywhere in the sim; unchanged).
- AI allies NEVER cast specials in A0.5 — specials are possession's payoff. (GAP: whether
  allies should eventually slam/volley is an A1 gambit question, parked.)

**Volley's delayed impact is the one new sim entity (shape corrected per review finding
F2):** `{owner, tiles, frames_left, damage}` — **a live `owner` reference** (take_hit
attribution, kill credit, and the possessed-only hitstop check all need the object), and a
**`frames_left` countdown decremented inside `tick_world`** — NEVER an absolute impact
frame, because hitstop advances `@frame` while freezing the sim (an absolute frame either
skips with `==` or fires early with `>=`; the projectile countdown is the proven pattern).
Impacts tick in a `tick_impacts` step ordered before `resolve_attacks` (creation order =
resolution order). Fire-and-forget: caster death does not cancel it (projectile precedent).
**Cleared in `enter_zone`** alongside `@projectiles` — one hook point that already covers
both zone transitions and wipe respawn (verified: `respawn_pack` calls `enter_zone`).

## The pack mark (the teamwork verb)

- **One key.** Sets the pack's mark: the hostile the possessed **last damaged** if alive and
  within `mark_range` (6 tiles); else the **nearest** hostile within `mark_range`;
  roster-order tiebreak. No valid target → refusal (no state change). Deterministic by
  construction.
- **Pack-owned state** (not creature-owned): survives voluntary and forced swaps — it is an
  order to the pack, not a property of a body. Cleared on: target death, re-mark,
  `enter_zone` (covers transition + wipe), or leash break (below).
- **AI change — honest statement (review finding F3; the draft's "WHO never WHEN" was
  wrong):** the mark overrides target SELECTION **and the aggro gate** — allies engage the
  marked target even outside their own `aggro_tiles`, and follow-the-possessed is suspended
  while a mark is live. That is the point: the mark is an ORDER. What bounds it:
  **`mark_leash_tiles` (14, hypothesis)** — the mark auto-clears when the target is farther
  than the leash from the POSSESSED body, so a surviving runner can never park both allies
  across the district, and an unreachable target (flow field returns no step) degrades to
  allies standing at the obstruction for at most the leash duration, not forever. On clear,
  allies revert to husk-grade nearest-hostile/follow. Chase, surround slots, retreat_step
  unchanged.
- Possessed body is unaffected (player-controlled). Humans cannot be ordered.
- **Wiring note (review finding FC):** `PossessedController#tick` already receives a `view`
  param (currently unused); mark flows through it (`view.set_mark(...)`), guarded so
  existing tests passing `nil` views stay green.
- **Render:** mark glyph over the target (vision-check line below).

## Controls

Home-row extension of the shipped scheme, both hands mirrored like attack/dodge:
**L / E = special · ; / Q = mark** (WASD/arrows move · J/Space attack · K/Shift dodge ·
Tab swap · Esc quit). Both new verbs are **edge-triggered post-swap** — added to
`PossessedController::EDGE_TRIGGERED` (law 2 semantics; a held special never ghost-casts
across a forced swap; held movement still survives per M2.1). Layout note (review finding
F6): `Gosu::KB_SEMICOLON` exists but SDL scancodes are positional — on non-US layouts the
physical key differs; Q is the layout-safe alternate, and BINDINGS stays the single
rebind point.

## The provenance law (review finding F8 — the biggest miss in the draft)

**A voluntary Tab is REFUSED while the possessed is mid-special (windup or active).**
Mirrors the existing stagger refusal in `World#handle_swap`. Rationale: the fun thesis
encourages cast → Tab, but a cast ABANDONED by a swap becomes an off-camera ally action —
hitstop attribution flips mid-flight, the camera leaves the impact, and the payoff goes
weightless; "last damaged" for the mark gets contaminated too. Refusing Tab for the 10-26
frames of windup+active makes each cast a **commitment beat** (cast completes, THEN you
swap), which also rate-limits the triple-cast burst without any cooldown hack. Recovery
frames do NOT block Tab (the hit has landed; leaving during follow-through is fine — and
it is exactly the cast-then-swap rhythm the thesis wants). Forced swap still cancels the
cast (death costs the cast, no refund — unchanged). Volley impacts already in flight are
fire-and-forget and unaffected.

## HUD

Each of the three bars gains a **special-ready pip** (you plan swaps around OTHER bodies'
readiness, so all three must read). This is the only HUD change. The A0 HUD law amends to:
"Three HP bars + exhaust pip + special pip per bar. Nothing else."

## Determinism & harness (Rule 2, blocking as always)

- Zero RNG introduced: Volley pattern fixed, mark selection deterministic, Lunge reuses the
  deterministic dash-through. All new state frame-quantized and sim-owned.
- Harness input schema grows **`special` + `mark` lanes** (same edge-trigger-after-swap
  semantics as the swap lane).
- New gate script **`specials_chain.json`**: full Slam → swap → Volley → swap → Lunge chain +
  a mark-converge sequence, every assertion-bearing beat on-camera for the possessed viewport
  (A0 camera law). Existing `world_loop.json` + `district_hunt.json` stay untouched as
  regression gates.
- New vision checks (added, never weakening existing 13): special effects legible and distinct
  per kit · Volley telegraph distinct from human telegraph AND from gate tiles · mark glyph
  readable · three special pips present and tracking.
- `rake perf` stays blocking; Volley impacts and mark checks are O(small-N) per tick.

## Edge cases (decided now so tests don't guess)

- Forced swap mid-special-windup: the dying body's cast dies with it (state machine is
  creature-owned); no refund — losing a body mid-cast is part of the cost. (Voluntary Tab
  can't create this case — the provenance law refuses it.)
- Special vs hitstop: casts freeze/resume exactly like attacks (hitstop pauses `tick_body`);
  Volley's `frames_left` countdown pauses with the sim by construction.
- Volley impacts landing after caster death: land anyway (fire-and-forget, above).
- Mark target dies to an ally mid-converge: mark clears; allies revert to nearest (no
  auto-re-mark — re-marking is the player's verb).
- Lunge into a wall mid-path: stops before the wall (dash-through wall law), damages only
  humans actually crossed.
- Lunge with zero free landing tile: refuses like dodge (no cast, no exhaust burn).
- All three specials on cooldown + owner mashing L: refusal is silent (no error feel); the
  pip IS the feedback.
- Slam/Volley multi-victim resolution order: RING order / tile-list order; victim N's
  knockback may change victim N+1's blocked list — order-dependent but deterministic
  (fixed iteration, review finding FF). A knocked-back human landing on a respawn tile is
  safe (respawns defer on occupied tiles, M2 invariant).

## Risks (named up front, per Rule 1)

1. **Self-sufficiency trap** — specials strong enough could erode ally relevance and the swap
   itself. Counters: long staggered exhausts keep basic attack + swap as bread-and-butter;
   the mark makes allies the sustained-damage channel; fun-verify question asks about the
   CHAIN, not the casts.
2. **Volley geometry (review finding F5)** — 40 sim-frames of delay vs a 16f rusher step
   means a converging target moves ~2.5 tiles before impact; Volley aimed at a runner
   misses by design. Its honest use is area denial on the surround ring (rushers
   converging ON the pack walk INTO it) — the fun-verify watches whether impacts ever
   land, and the delay constant is the tuning knob if not.
3. **Power inflation vs M2.1's balance** — specials at these numbers trivialize current rusher
   waves. Owned deliberately: A0.5 is the power-fantasy beat after M2.1's fairness beat;
   pressure re-tunes in A1+ (Shooters, pull economy) — do NOT pre-nerf specials to protect
   today's difficulty (Vlambeer: err on power for the verify).
4. **Rotation-homework risk (review finding FA)** — mitigated by staggered exhausts
   (480/600/720) + the provenance law's commitment beat; the fun-verify explicitly asks
   whether the chain felt situational or rote.
