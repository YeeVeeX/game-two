# A0.5 — Kit specials + pack mark (spec)

**Date:** 2026-08-09 · **Status:** drafted post-brainstorm, pending dual adversarial review
(Codex cross-vendor + Fable 5 max) · **Predecessor:** A0 possession core
(`2026-08-09-a0-possession-core-design.md`, fun-verified through M2.1 merge `0c2f9ba`).
**Binding upstream:** the 5 design laws (`docs/design-corpus/design-review-reconciliation.md`),
the A0 spec's combat laws, the de-slop rule, and the M2.1-shipped feel invariants.

## Why this increment (owner directive, 2026-08-09)

Owner verdict after fun-verifying M2.1: *"feels better now, yeah, now lets work on the combat
mechanics itself, add some spells and methods of teamwork."* Brainstorm converged on:
**one special ability per kit** (big-moment cadence, ~10s) designed to chain ACROSS bodies via
the swap, plus **one pack command** (focus-target mark). Gambit engine stays A1. This is A0.5:
smaller than a full A-increment, bigger than a tune.

**Fun thesis:** *the swap becomes a combo system you can feel.* Special exhausts are
per-creature and swap-inert (law 4), so the natural line is cast → Tab → cast → Tab → cast — a
three-beat the architecture already supports invisibly. Specials make it visible and loud;
the mark makes the allies part of the plan instead of scenery.

## Naming discipline (de-slop rule, owner-set)

Slam / Volley / Lunge / mark are **internal spec-speak**, never player-visible. The fiction
order form at the end carries the naming asks to the bible session. Slop test applies.

## The three specials

All numbers are **hypotheses** → `data/balance/combat.json` under `kits.<kit>.special`
(zero constants in code, non-negotiable #3). 600f = 10s at the 60fps tick.

| | Blocker: **Slam** | Lobber: **Volley** | Striker: **Lunge** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role in the chain | Opener — control | Zone denial — punish | Finisher — execute |
| Effect | Hits all 8 ring tiles; 2-tile knockback; 30f stagger on every human hit | 3 impact tiles at distances 2/3/4 along facing; telegraphed 40f; then AoE damage per tile | Dashes 4 tiles THROUGH bodies (reuses M2.1 `commit_through`); damages every human crossed; i-frames for the dash |
| Damage | 30 (control, not kill — blocker identity) | 35 per tile | 50 (one-shots a rusher — the finisher fantasy) |
| Cast frames | windup 12 / active 4 / recovery 12 | windup 10 / recovery 12 (impact is remote) | windup 6 / dash 4f-per-tile |
| Exhaust | 600f | 600f | 600f |

**Casting rules (uniform):**
- Special enters the existing windup→active→recovery state machine with its own frame counts —
  stagger blocks it, `interrupt_on_hit` interrupts it (striker/lobber yes, blocker no: kit
  identity carries over), and it cannot start unless `attack_state == :idle`.
- **Special exhaust is a SECOND per-creature clock**, independent of attack exhaust —
  creature-owned, swap-inert, ticks while unpossessed (law 4 verbatim). Basic attacks stay
  available while special recharges.
- Specials hit hostiles only (no friendly fire anywhere in the sim; unchanged).
- One cast damages a given victim at most once (per-cast hit registry, same as per-swing).
- AI allies NEVER cast specials in A0.5 — specials are possession's payoff. (GAP: whether
  allies should eventually slam/volley is an A1 gambit question, parked.)

**Volley's delayed impact is the one new sim entity:** `{tiles, impact_frame, damage, owner
faction}` — spawned at cast, telegraph rendered from cast to impact, damage applied to any
hostile standing on an impact tile at the impact frame, then despawns. Fire-and-forget: caster
death does not cancel it (projectile precedent). Cleared on zone transition and wipe.

## The pack mark (the teamwork verb)

- **One key.** Sets the pack's mark: the hostile the possessed **last damaged** if alive and
  within `mark_range` (6 tiles); else the **nearest** hostile within `mark_range`;
  roster-order tiebreak. No valid target → refusal (no state change). Deterministic by
  construction.
- **Pack-owned state** (not creature-owned): survives voluntary and forced swaps — it is an
  order to the pack, not a property of a body. Cleared on: target death, re-mark, zone
  transition, wipe.
- **AI change (the whole diff):** in `AiController#tick`, a living marked target overrides
  `nearest(...)` for pack-faction creatures — allies converge and fight it regardless of their
  own aggro range, then revert to husk-grade nearest-hostile when the mark clears. Chase,
  surround slots, retreat_step all unchanged — the mark changes WHO, never HOW.
- Possessed body is unaffected (player-controlled). Humans cannot be ordered.
- **Render:** mark glyph over the target (vision-check line below).

## Controls

Home-row extension of the shipped scheme, both hands mirrored like attack/dodge:
**L / E = special · ; / Q = mark** (WASD/arrows move · J/Space attack · K/Shift dodge ·
Tab swap · Esc quit). Both new verbs are **edge-triggered post-swap** — added to
`PossessedController::EDGE_TRIGGERED` (law 2 semantics; a held special never ghost-casts
across a forced swap; held movement still survives per M2.1).

## HUD

Each of the three bars gains a **special-ready pip** (you plan swaps around OTHER bodies'
readiness, so all three must read). This is the only HUD change. The A0 HUD law amends to:
"Three HP bars + exhaust pip + special pip per bar. Nothing else."

## Determinism & harness (Rule 2, blocking as always)

- Zero RNG introduced: Volley pattern fixed, mark selection deterministic, Lunge reuses the
  deterministic dash-through. All new state frame-quantized and sim-owned.
- Harness input schema grows **`special` + `mark` lanes** (same edge-trigger-after-swap
  semantics as the swap lane).
- New gate script **`specials_chain.json`**: full Slam → swap → Volley → swap → Lunge chain +
  a mark-converge sequence, every assertion-bearing beat on-camera for the possessed viewport
  (A0 camera law). Existing `world_loop.json` + `district_hunt.json` stay untouched as
  regression gates.
- New vision checks (added, never weakening existing 13): special effects legible and distinct
  per kit · Volley telegraph distinct from human telegraph AND from gate tiles · mark glyph
  readable · three special pips present and tracking.
- `rake perf` stays blocking; Volley impacts and mark checks are O(small-N) per tick.

## Quantitative scope rails (gamesmith discipline — checkable numbers, not vibes)

3 specials · 1 pack command · **0** resource meters · **0** upgrade/progression hooks ·
**1** new sim entity type (Volley impact) · **1** new HUD element type (pip) · **2** new input
lanes · **2** new event symbols (`special_started`, `pack_mark_set` — registered on first use,

## Build order (kill-cheap: riskiest-cheapest probe first, gamesmith GATE-0 discipline)

1. **Mark** (pure AI targeting change + one event + glyph — cheapest, and it is the teamwork
   CLAIM of the increment; if converge-on-mark doesn't visibly change a fight, stop and
   rethink before specials exist).
2. **Slam** (reuses ring arc + knockback + stagger machinery end-to-end).
3. **Lunge** (reuses dash-through; adds crossed-tile damage).
4. **Volley** (the one new entity — built last, when the pattern is proven).
5. Special-exhaust clock + HUD pips + harness lanes ride with their first consumer;
   `specials_chain.json` + vision checks close the increment.

Each step: test-per-fix, commit-per-task, suite green before the next.

## Edge cases (decided now so tests don't guess)

- Forced swap mid-special-windup: the dying body's cast dies with it (state machine is
  creature-owned); no refund — losing a body mid-cast is part of the cost.
- Special vs hitstop: casts freeze/resume exactly like attacks (hitstop pauses `tick_body`).
- Volley impacts landing after caster death: land anyway (fire-and-forget, above).
- Mark target dies to an ally mid-converge: mark clears; allies revert to nearest (no
  auto-re-mark — re-marking is the player's verb).
- Lunge into a wall mid-path: stops before the wall (dash-through wall law), damages only
  humans actually crossed.
- Lunge with zero free landing tile: refuses like dodge (no cast, no exhaust burn).
- All three specials on cooldown + owner mashing L: refusal is silent (no error feel); the
  pip IS the feedback.

## Risks (named up front, per Rule 1)

1. **Self-sufficiency trap** — specials strong enough could erode ally relevance and the swap
   itself. Counters: 600f exhaust keeps basic attack + swap as bread-and-butter; the mark
   makes allies the sustained-damage channel; fun-verify question asks about the CHAIN, not
   the casts.
2. **Volley scope** — delayed impacts are the only new entity; kept to one fixed pattern, one
   delay constant, built last (kill-cheap order).
3. **Power inflation vs M2.1's balance** — specials at these numbers trivialize current rusher
   waves. Owned deliberately: A0.5 is the power-fantasy beat after M2.1's fairness beat;
   pressure re-tunes in A1+ (Shooters, pull economy) — do NOT pre-nerf specials to protect
   today's difficulty (Vlambeer: err on power for the verify).
