# A0.6 — Blocker taunt ("exeta res" analog)

Status: REVISED (post-review, 2026-08-10). Owner-promoted 2026-08-10 (scope
contract v5). Adversarial review: 3 lenses (code-fit, design, fun), 18 findings,
0 fatal, all folded — verdicts and fold ledger in `drafts/_a06-spec-review.md`.
Owner ask, verbatim: *"the 'tank' is too weak, and should get more aggro from the
enemies or have an 'exeta res'-like spell to pull aggro."*

## Reference wall

- **Tibia `exeta res` (knight challenge):** instant cast, creatures in range switch
  target to the caster for a duration. The knight's whole party role in one button.
- **The shipped A0.5 mark:** symmetry law — *mark orders allies onto one target;
  taunt orders enemies onto one body; and your own taunt binds YOU* (design
  decision 5). Same possessed-only cast discipline, same data-driven range/duration
  shape, same "readable order, no invisible math" doctrine.
- **Vlambeer juice:** the cast needs a visible pulse; the state needs a persistent tell.

## The problem, precisely

The sim has **no aggro system**: humans target the nearest hostile
(`AiController#nearest`) every tick — and because `nearest` tie-breaks by roster
index and the roster is [striker, blocker, lobber], **every distance tie goes to the
striker**: the tank structurally loses ties to the exact body you swap to protect
(review C4). The blocker's 160 HP buys nothing because standing in front of allies
does not make enemies choose it — "more aggro" would mean inventing invisible threat
weighting the player can't read. The readable, Tibia-faithful fix is a **taunt
verb**: one press, humans in range re-target the blocker's BODY for a fixed duration.

## Design decisions (dev-of-record calls, defended)

### 1. Verb slot: taunt rides Slam's cast — no new key, no rail break

Options weighed: (a) second special on a new key — breaks the shipped ONE-special-
per-kit rail, adds a binding and HUD pip; (b) replace Slam — deletes an identity the
owner just fun-verified in A0.5; (c) **fold taunt into Slam's active entry** — the
ring shockwave also *provokes* everyone who feels it. (c) wins: zero new controls,
kit grammar intact, and the coupling IS the tank fantasy — you cannot announce
yourself quietly. Casting Slam mid-room now means "the room turns on me": possess
blocker → wade in → Slam → swap to striker → carve. That sequence is the monster-flip
pack-play thesis in one breath.

Mechanically: the taunt pulse fires **once, at active entry** (first active frame),
consuming the `action_can_trigger?`/`action_triggered!` one-shot — which the ring
arc does NOT use today (`resolve_tile_action` resolves every active frame with
per-victim dedup via `action_can_hit?`); the flag is free on ring kits, and
consuming it for the pulse cannot affect ring damage (review A, verified). Radius
`taunt.range_tiles` (Chebyshev) — WIDER than the ring's 8 adjacent damage tiles.
Slam = close damage + wide provocation.

**Honesty note (review B2):** the pulse fires whether or not the ring hits anyone —
an air-Slam at range 5 taunts the room *without* wading in. The damage-coupling is
optional; the **exhaust-coupling is the real cost model**: a whiffed cast burns the
full 600f clock. The ranged pull is a legal opener, not a bug.

⚠️ **Recorded touchstone tension (gamesmith Tibia extract, 2026-08-10):** the real
exeta res is cast *repeatedly* in-fight — the knight re-challenges every few
seconds; only the ~0.6s global exhaustion gates it. Riding Slam's 600f (~10s)
exhaust makes our taunt ~one-per-fight: 300f locked, then a ≥300f trough in which
targeting reverts to `nearest` — the trough IS the pre-A0.6 game (review C2).
Shipping the coupled version FIRST is deliberate (zero new controls, one variable).
**Measurable decouple trigger:** if fun-verify Q3 says the gap feels starved, OR a
pack member dies during a taunt trough in an ordinary 3-pull during fun-verify,
the fix is **same key, two clocks** — L always pulses the taunt on its own shorter
exhaust (~360f) while Slam's damage keeps the 600f clock — never a duration/HP
buff, and never a second binding (keeps the out-of-scope list honest, review B2).
See `drafts/_gamesmith-touchstone-digest.md` §5.

Data-generic within its arc: any **ring-arc** special carrying a `taunt` block
pulses; the other arcs (dash/projectile/volley) already consume the one-shot for
their launch and would need a per-arc hook — out of scope (review A2). Obedience is
human-AI-side only: pack husks ignore locks on them and a possessed body cannot be
compelled (controller reads input, not state) — humans-only until A1 (review B5).
Zero balance constants in code.

### 2. Duration: independent 300f, decoupled from the 45f stagger

`taunt.duration_frames = 300` (5s at 60tps). Derivation (review C5 — replaces the
earlier respawn-beat claim, which was numerology): 300f ≈ the striker's realistic
3–4-kill clearance of a median pull (25 dmg @ 35f exhaust = 2-tap per 50 HP rusher,
~60–75f per kill with repositioning) ≈ the blocker's unpeeled survival bound at
N≤3 attackers (see the fuse, decision 6). Range 6 = `mark_range_tiles` — the
mark/taunt symmetry law in the data itself. Note: range 6 < aggro 10, so taunt
never *acquires* an enemy — everything in range was already aggroed; it is purely
a re-target + lock.

The Slam-hit 45f stagger is the *victim's verb lock*; taunt is *targeting*.
Independent clocks, both creature-owned, both decayed in `tick_body` (hitstop/veil
pause them exactly like stagger — determinism law, verified in review A).

### 3. Taunted humans ignore the aggro distance gate (like marked allies do)

While taunted, a human engages the taunter regardless of `aggro_tiles` — the lock is
the point (they ignore your squishies AND distance). It ends by: **expiry**, the
**taunter's death** (tank falls → the room turns on you — fiction-coherent), or a
**fresh taunt** overwriting it. Death is a RELEASE, not a suspension: `taunted_target`
lazily clears the victim's state when it observes the taunter dead — otherwise a
pack-wipe revival resurrects locks the blocker never re-cast (review A1; lazy clear
is the only option since victims in abandoned zones don't tick). Chase uses the
existing generic path (`surround_slot` + flow field), so taunted humans pincer the
blocker exactly as they pincer anything.

Recorded silent calls (review B4): **no line of sight** — a shockwave is felt
through a wall, and mark is equally LoS-free; **no leash** — deliberately
asymmetric with mark's 14-tile leash, because the lock expiring is the leash;
**the gate-pull is legal** — taunt, step through a gate, return: the remaining
lock beelines the room to your prepared position. That is the Tibia pull in
miniature, costs a 600f Slam, and self-limits (they converge on the slowest body
in the game). A2's pull economy inherits it deliberately.

### 4. Taunt is a player decision (possessed-only by construction)

`AiController#engage` never calls `start_special`, so an unpossessed blocker cannot
taunt — same discipline as mark. Gambit-driven AI taunting is A1 territory.

### 5. The anchor holds: your own taunt binds you (review B1 — the HIGH)

The intended play hands the anchor to husk AI, and husk AI *walks*: a live pack
mark drags it across the map (mark has no distance gate), and with no hostile in
aggro range it follows the possessed — marching the taunted knot onto exactly the
body you swapped to protect. One targeting clause fixes all drift cases: pack-side,
**a creature with living taunt victims targets its nearest living taunt victim —
above mark, no aggro gate** — until the lock expires or breaks. The symmetry law
completes itself: mark binds allies, taunt binds enemies, and your own taunt binds
YOU — you screamed at the room; you hold the line for 300f. Consequence (accepted,
fiction-coherent, self-expiring): mark cannot call the blocker off mid-taunt. No
new AI mode, no gambits, no data.

### 6. The fuse is intended (review C3)

Post-Slam arithmetic (all from combat.json): victims are knocked 2 tiles + 45f
staggered; first re-hit lands ≈ t=97, then one window per 66f → max 4 hits × 12 dmg
per rusher per lock. A fresh 160 HP blocker survives N≤3 attackers unpeeled; N=4
dies at ≈ expiry; N=5 dies at ≈ t=229. With the striker carving from t≈70, N=3
leaves the blocker at ~50 HP. **Taunt survivability assumes the swap-carve — a
watched taunt at N≥4 kills the blocker by design.** The blocker's remaining HP is
a fuse the striker races; a dead blocker breaks the lock AND leaves the pack
tankless until full wipe. That is the tension, not a defect.

### 7. Staging: one clustered trio in the district (review C1 — the other HIGH)

The current district cannot stage the fantasy: no two rusher spawns are within one
taunt radius of each other (closest pair Chebyshev 10), so the median cast taunts
ONE rusher — a verb no ordinary fight can showcase fails "every commit changes what
the player feels." Fold: **cluster the (30,18) spawn into a trio** — add two spawns
adjacent to it ([32,18] and [32,17], verified passable; the draft's [31,20] is a
wall) in `data/zones/district.json`. Far corner, away from the entry-gate paths
the four existing gate scripts walk; **all four existing gates must re-verify green
after the zone edit** (their event beats, not just vision hatches). This is staging
for the verb, not D0 tuning (no drop/decay/station change); the fun-verify cannot
isolate a verb it never showcases.

## Sim spec

- **Creature** (state is on the VICTIM — law 4, creature-owned, swap-inert):
  `taunt!(taunter, frames)` sets `@taunted_by`/`@taunt_frames`;
  `taunted_target` → the taunter while frames > 0 and taunter alive; observing a
  dead taunter **clears** the state (release, not suspension — review A1);
  `@taunt_frames` decays in `tick_body`; `revive!` clears it. Possession swaps touch
  none of it — the lock is to the blocker's *body*, not the possession pointer
  (swapping away is the intended play).
- **AiController** (humans): target priority becomes
  `taunted_target || nearest(...)`; the engage gate treats a taunted target like a
  mark (no `aggro_tiles` check). Pack-side (design decision 5): a creature with
  living taunt victims targets its nearest living taunt victim, above mark, no
  aggro gate.
- **World#resolve_attacks**, ring arc: when `cfg[:taunt]` and
  `attacker.action_can_trigger?` → `action_triggered!`, every living hostile within
  `range_tiles` (Chebyshev) gets `taunt!(attacker, duration_frames)`, emit
  `:taunted` (event registered on first use) with `actor:` + `victims:` count,
  record a cosmetic pulse `{tile:, frames_left:}` for the renderer. Pulses tick in
  `tick_world` (hitstop-paused like impacts) and are **cleared in `enter_zone`**
  (projectile/impact precedent — else a knockback shove through a gate mid-pulse
  ghosts the ring onto the new zone's map, review A3). Ring damage resolution is
  unchanged and still per-victim.
- **Zone edges:** humans tick only in the current zone, so a taunted human in an
  abandoned zone freezes with its remaining frames (existing law for all timers);
  a ticking victim is always co-zoned with a living taunter (whole-pack transit —
  review A verified); the dead-taunter case is covered by the lazy clear.

## Presentation spec (Rule 2 surface)

- **Victim tell (persistent):** a 3px underline in the blocker's body color (deep
  rust, 190/80/35) beneath each taunted human, **pinned at y + SIZE + 5** — clear
  of the telegraph swell, which floods to y + SIZE + 4 in near-identical hot red
  and would otherwise erase the tell in exactly the modal frame (mid-taunt, human
  attacking — review B3). Distinct from the red-edge telegraph (a surround flare),
  the white-family hurt flash (a body fill), the teal mark reticle (corners), and
  drop magenta. **Expiry telegraph:** the underline's alpha fades over the final
  third of `duration_frames` — the lock snapping back to `nearest` is the striker's
  most dangerous moment, and drop decay already taught this exact grammar.
- **Cast tell (momentary):** one continuous **expanding hollow rust SQUARE
  outline** from the blocker out to taunt range over `taunt.pulse_frames` —
  square because range is Chebyshev (a circle under-reads the corners by 2+
  tiles) and because per-tile marks would read as volley brackets in a static
  capture (review B3). Rust hue = blocker body color (ownership semantics).
- **No HUD change.** The special pip already tracks Slam's exhaust; taunt adds no
  numeral (quiet-HUD law).
- Fiction order form: "taunt"/"Slam" remain internal spec-speak; player-visible
  names await the bible.

## Harness + gates

- **Baseline falsifier — RUN, thesis CONFIRMED (2026-08-10):** pilot flight
  `taunt_baseline` (SEED=11) measured retarget latency ≈ **14–17 frames** across
  five independent contacts (bound: ≤90f confirms, >240f falsifies) — the
  tie-break flips onto the striker essentially on contact; the striker husk died
  in two of three sweeps while the 160 HP blocker took only chip. "Tank too
  weak" is now a number. Evidence + methodology in
  `drafts/_a06-spec-review.md` (baseline result section); export at
  `tmp/pilot/taunt_baseline/baseline.json`.
- New gate script `taunt_anchor.json`, **authored via pilot mode**: fly
  possess-blocker → wade → Slam → **wait ≥16f** (the swap is refused while
  `special_committed?` — windup 12 + active 4 — and the held key's edge is then
  swallowed; verify the export contains `possession_changed` before trusting the
  script — review A5) → swap → carve, **and press mark during the taunt window**
  (the B1 anchor rule is only exercised if the two order-verbs collide in the
  gate). Aim captures at cast / mid-taunt convergence / expiry-fade.
- APPENDED vision checks (existing 20 never weaken), pass-true hatches for scripts
  that don't exercise taunt:
  1. `taunt_underline_reads` — taunted humans carry a rust underline BELOW the
     body (offset clear of the telegraph flare), distinct from telegraph/hurt/
     mark/drops; near expiry it reads faded.
  2. `taunt_pulse_reads` — the cast frame shows one expanding hollow rust SQUARE
     outline centered on the blocker, distinct from Slam's damage tiles, the
     volley brackets, and the human telegraph.
  3. `taunt_convergence_reads` — mid-taunt frame: multiple humans adjacent
     to/facing the rust-underlined cluster around the blocker while the possessed
     (white-ringed) body stands apart, not swarmed.
- Tests (minitest, real World, no mocks): taunt state set/decay/dead-taunter
  clears (not just nils); revive clears; **wipe→respawn→re-enter does NOT
  resurrect the lock** (review A1); AI priority + distance-gate bypass;
  **tie-break delta** — rusher equidistant (d=1) to striker and blocker targets
  the STRIKER un-taunted and the BLOCKER taunted (review C4); **anchor rule** —
  husk blocker with living victims ignores mark and follow (review B1); pulse
  fires ONCE per cast at active entry; range boundary (6 in, 7 out); event
  emission; re-taunt overwrites; ring damage unchanged; taunted chase survives
  possession swap; taunter death releases the lock; pulse records cleared on
  zone entry.

## Out of scope (parking lot, not code)

Threat/aggro accumulation systems (A2's soft-cap economy is the successor), AI
blockers taunting (A1 gambits), taunt breaking on damage-to-caster, diminishing
returns, any second special or new binding (the recorded decouple fallback stays
on the SAME key — decision 1), D0 tuning (waits for post-A0.6 re-verify).

## Fun-verify (owner questions, asked after ship)

1. Does possessing the blocker now feel like playing a TANK — did Slam-then-swap
   become a move you *wanted* to make?
2. Did fights get stickier in a good way (enemies committed to the anchor) or an
   annoying way (too locked, no counterplay)?
3. How did the RHYTHM feel — 5 seconds of lock, then ~5 seconds where the room
   unlocks before Slam is back: is the gap between taunts too long, too short,
   or the interesting part?
4. Did the blocker die while taunting — and did that feel like your mistake or
   the game's?
